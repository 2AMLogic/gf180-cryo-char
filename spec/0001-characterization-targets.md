# 0001 — GF180 port of the NIST MPW-5 characterization tile

Status: RATIFIED on merge

The rows below take effect on merge after independent EE and market review and operator approval of the same revision. A push starts those reviews over.

## Decision

Build a GF180MCU-D full-slot characterization die on the wafer.space template. Preserve the source structure identifiers so that a measurement on the GF180 die can be joined to the NIST reference by identifier, geometry, bias and temperature. Device technology, legal drawn dimensions and well connections differ between processes. Report those differences with the result. The source geometry is derived from the published workbook and module descriptions. Use only the GF180 PDK and generators for the new layout.

The mapping, pin assignments and numeric budgets are part of this decision. Stretch work on additional devices requires a later decision and independent evidence. The target is room-temperature modelling and comparison; cryogenic behaviour is a future silicon measurement. The single-gate simulation exceeds the 5 fA design target before pad contributions. The independent foundry pad screen reaches 107.45 pA of absolute clamp current at FF/125 °C/3.6 V. Guarding and switch sizing leave these pad junctions connected. An uncorrected 10 fA measurement at that hot pad bias fails even with an ideal mux. A corrected claim needs physical pad-mismatch and drift bounds at its stated temperature and bias. The complete multiplexer, secondary pad protection and sensitivity of the physical measurement path still await verification. A cut to meet the shuttle date stays visible in the Bronze tracker.
